Measure an incline

  1. Stand the phone on its charging-port edge with the screen facing the side, so the phone's width runs along the slope. Turn it the same way every time. Leaning sideways doesn't matter.
  2. Tap Auto measure, put the phone on the floor and let go. When the frame flashes green, move it to the next spot.
  3. Or measure by hand: Set zero on the reference surface, then Save reading when the frame is green.
